# Research Question

Does assigning a detailed professional persona produce higher-quality AI-generated marketing content than assigning only a simple role?

---

# Hypothesis

Providing a detailed persona—including professional experience, writing style, expertise, and communication goals—will produce marketing content that is more persuasive, audience-focused, and strategically written than assigning only a generic professional role.

---

# Variables

## Independent Variable

Prompting technique.

- Prompt A: Role Prompting
- Prompt B: Persona Prompting

## Dependent Variable

Quality of the generated LinkedIn marketing post.

# Analysis

## Prompt A

Role Prompting produced professional and well-structured LinkedIn posts across all three AI models. The responses followed the requested format, communicated the platform's benefits clearly, and included effective calls-to-action.

However, the outputs primarily focused on describing product features and promotional messaging.

---

## Prompt B

Persona Prompting consistently produced more persuasive and audience-aware marketing content.

Rather than simply describing the product, the models framed the marketing message around customer challenges before introducing NovaSpark AI as the solution.

The assigned persona influenced not only the writing style but also the reasoning process used to construct the marketing narrative.

---

# Cross-Model Observations

### ChatGPT

- Produced warmer and more customer-focused messaging.
- Improved engagement through stronger hooks and conversational language.
- Generated more natural calls-to-action than Role Prompting.

### Gemini

- Produced the strongest strategic business messaging.
- Focused on customer pain points before presenting product features.
- Emphasised measurable business value and practical outcomes.

### Claude

- Demonstrated the strongest persona adoption.
- Wrote directly from the assigned strategist's perspective.
- Produced highly authentic B2B marketing copy with consultative language and persuasive storytelling.

---

# Findings

- Persona Prompting improved audience awareness across all tested models.
- Responses became more persuasive by focusing on business problems before introducing product features.
- The assigned persona influenced communication strategy in addition to writing style.
- Claude demonstrated the strongest persona adoption, while Gemini excelled in strategic messaging and ChatGPT produced highly approachable customer-focused content.
- Persona Prompting consistently generated more authentic B2B marketing content than simple Role Prompting.

---

# Conclusion

Persona Prompting significantly improved AI-generated marketing content compared with simple Role Prompting.

Across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude, assigning a detailed professional persona resulted in stronger audience targeting, more persuasive messaging, improved brand voice consistency, and more natural business communication.

These findings suggest that Persona Prompting influences both the style and strategic reasoning of large language models.
