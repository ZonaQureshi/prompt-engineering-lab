# Research Question

Does providing a structured marketing brief produce more effective AI-generated marketing content than a simple prompt?

---

# Hypothesis

Providing detailed marketing context—including the target audience, objective, brand voice, and content requirements—will produce marketing content that is more persuasive, targeted, and aligned with business goals than a simple prompt.

---

# Variables

## Independent Variable

Prompt structure.

- Prompt A: Simple marketing request.
- Prompt B: Structured marketing brief.

## Dependent Variable

Quality of the generated Instagram marketing content.

## Controlled Variables

- Same business (Chai Khana)
- Same social media platform (Instagram)
- Same task
- Same AI models
- Same evaluation rubric

---

# Business Scenario

**Business:** Chai Khana

**Objective**

Increase weekday afternoon visits among university students and young professionals.

# Analysis

## Prompt A

The simple prompt generated a general promotional post suitable for Instagram but lacked a defined target audience and business objective. The content could easily apply to almost any café and did not specifically encourage weekday afternoon visits.

## Prompt B

The structured marketing brief produced significantly stronger content. Both ChatGPT and Gemini generated posts tailored to university students and young professionals, incorporated the requested brand voice, highlighted relevant features such as specialty beverages and free Wi-Fi, and included effective calls to action.

Both models closely followed the marketing objective while maintaining a consistent and engaging tone.

---

# Findings

- Marketing context substantially improved content quality.
- Defining the target audience increased message relevance.
- Specifying the business objective produced more focused promotional content.
- Explicit brand voice instructions improved consistency.
- Structured prompts generated stronger calls to action.
- Both ChatGPT and Gemini showed similar improvements, demonstrating that the technique transfers across different AI models.

---

# Conclusion

Marketing Prompt Engineering significantly improved AI-generated marketing content.

Compared with a simple prompt, the structured marketing brief produced outputs that were more persuasive, audience-focused, and aligned with business objectives.

Across both ChatGPT and Gemini, Prompt B consistently generated more professional marketing copy suitable for real-world promotional use.
